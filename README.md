# Twin Courtyard Estate II

[Open the interactive walkthrough](https://mpc0367.github.io/twin-courtyard-estate-walkthrough/)

Two courtyard houses, a garden pavilion and a seven-car garage in Krungthep Kreetha, Bangkok. The viewer runs in a modern desktop or mobile browser with WebGL 2.

Choose **Walk** and use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Drag to look around. On a touchscreen, use the directional pad. Approach a door, the garage shutter or an estate gate and press **E**, or tap its prompt, to open or close it. **Render quality** enables enhanced materials, reflections and contact shadows.
